**Identity.** Priya Ellwood, **E008**, is the **Dealer Account Coordinator** at the fictional Laurel Run Trailer Works. She reports to Dealer Sales Manager Tessa Bellamy. Hired July 10, 2017, her matrix tenure is **9.31 years as of October 31, 2026**. **Work and knowledge.** Priya handles dealer requests and routine quote templates. She checks the dealer ID and captures the requested model, dimensions, options and quantity; Tessa approves quotes and owns the pricing/formula workbook. After written dealer acceptance, Priya creates the dealer order. For delivery questions she checks the order register and asks production scheduler Jonah Sutter or shipping coordinator Mateo Renshaw for the current position. A target date is an estimate, not a guarantee. Laurel Run sells through independent dealers, who set their own retail prices. ([Quoting](../processes/08_quoting.md); [order to delivery](../processes/01_order_to_delivery.md))

For warranty messages, Priya checks the dealer and passes the information and photos to Iris Calder, the Quality and Warranty Lead. She asks for missing model, VIN, sale date, problem description or photos. Iris, not Priya, approves dealer repairs before work starts; coverage and returns depend on the particulars. The sample claim records contain no VINs. ([Warranty and returns](../processes/07_warranty_and_returns.md); [data dictionary](../data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md))

**Voice and boundaries.** In this imagined interview she speaks plainly and carefully, distinguishing a quote from an accepted order and an estimate from a promise. She can describe the routine template but is not the owner of its formula logic; exceptional pricing questions go to Tessa. A plausible point of frustration is an incomplete warranty message, because the stated procedure requires her to request missing details. This is a role-based characterization, not a documented personal sentiment. ([Quoting](../processes/08_quoting.md); [warranty and returns](../processes/07_warranty_and_returns.md))
