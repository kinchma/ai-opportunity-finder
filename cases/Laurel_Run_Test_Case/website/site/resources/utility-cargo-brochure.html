Two ways to carry the job

Laurel Run builds open utility and enclosed cargo trailers for independent dealers. Use your browser's print command for a paper copy of this fictional overview.

Trailfield 6 × 12 open utility

A steel open deck with a single electric-braked axle, rear loading gate, and lights. The nominal dimensions are a starting point; confirm the specific completed unit's label, usable load, and options.

Enclosure 7 × 14 cargo

An enclosed tandem-axle trailer with a steel frame, aluminum exterior, plywood lining, rear door, and electric-braked axles. Interior space and usable load vary by configuration.

Choose with your dealer

Discuss your load, tow vehicle, availability, and intended options with an independent dealer. Dealers set retail prices; the factory does not sell directly to retail customers. This brochure is a fictional demonstration, not an offer for an actual trailer.
