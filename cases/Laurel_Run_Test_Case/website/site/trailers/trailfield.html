A practical open deck

The Trailfield is a straightforward utility trailer for everyday hauling. Its 6-foot by 12-foot open steel platform and rear loading gate give equipment and materials a place to ride without an enclosed body. The single axle has electric brakes. Think of it as a starting point for general-purpose jobs, with the trailer, load, and tow vehicle selected as a complete combination.

The axle rating is a component rating, not the trailer's gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) or its available payload. The completed unit's certification and load labels determine the applicable ratings; the trailer's own weight, installed equipment, and other factors affect what it can carry. Never infer payload from dimensions or an axle rating.

Plan for the load

Consider the shape and weight of what you intend to move, how it will be secured, the gate opening, and the tow vehicle and hitch ratings. A dealer can explain the available configuration and whether options such as a spare-tire mount or upgraded jack are offered for a particular unit. Confirm all details on the specific trailer before making a decision.
