# Research Note 2 - Maintenance Agreements and Renewal Rates

External context only, gathered October 2026.

## Renewal benchmarks

Contracting Business, an HVAC trade publication, recommends at least an 86% annual renewal rate for maintenance agreements. Source: https://www.contractingbusiness.com/residential-hvac/article/20868670/hvac-maintenance-agreements-by-the-numbers

Vendor guides commonly cite a 70% to 80% renewal target, with strong programs near 90%. Source: https://oxmaint.com/industries/hvac/hvac-service-contract-renewal-retention-tracking

ACHR News profiled a Pennsylvania contractor with about 1,600 agreements and an 83% renewal rate. Source: https://www.achrnews.com/articles/153291-contractors-share-maintenance-agreement-best-practices

## Why agreements lapse

Several vendor sources say renewals slip when reminders depend on someone running a manual export or remembering to call, and suggest scheduled reminders by text, email and phone. These claims come from companies selling automation and their lift figures are not independently verified.

## Scope benchmarks

Residential plans usually include two visits a year; commercial plans often four, guided by the ACCA Standard 4 maintenance scope. Source: https://www.smartservice.com/blog/how-much-hvac-service-agreements

## Limits

Renewal figures vary by residential versus commercial and by how a firm counts auto-renewing card plans.
