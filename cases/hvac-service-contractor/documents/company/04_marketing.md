# Marketing

Updated October 2026. Facts on what we do today.

## Channels

- Repeat customers and referrals. About 70% of residential customers have used us before. We do not track referral sources in QuickBooks; Nadia asks on install proposals and writes it on the folder.

- Website (larkmoorcomfort.example). Built in 2019 by a local web designer. Has a request form that goes to service@. Updated about once a year.

- Google Business Profile. 4.3 stars from 112 reviews at the snapshot. Jolene answers reviews when she sees them. We do not ask customers for reviews in any regular way; some techs ask happy customers, most do not.

- Facebook page, about 1,900 followers. Jolene posts when she has time: 9 posts in the reporting year (see data/marketing_activity.csv). No Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok or YouTube accounts.

- Comfort Club postcards mailed to all members each February and August, about $1,100 per mailing.

- Local radio spots in January and July, about $4,500 per campaign. Glen decides.

- Truck wraps on all 19 vehicles.

## Budget

About $38,000 for the year, set by Glen. Radio and postcards are most of it.

## What is measured

- Number of web form requests (Dana counts them at month-end from service@): about 410 in the year.
- Google rating and review count (Jolene checks now and then).
- We do not measure cost per lead, which channel a customer came from (outside Nadia's notes on installs), or Facebook results.
