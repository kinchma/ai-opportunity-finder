# Payroll and Timesheets

Owner: Ruth Vossberg. Backup: none named (Dana has seen it done). Last updated April 2019.

## Purpose

Pay all 35 employees correctly every other Friday through ADP RUN, including overtime and on-call pay for the field staff.

## Timesheets

- Field staff (24): paper weekly timesheet. Each day: start time, lunch, end time, job numbers worked, on-call hours, and overtime. Signed by the employee. Service techs give them to Sam; installers give them to Ben. Sam and Ben initial them and put them in Ruth's tray on Monday morning.
- Office staff (11): sign-in sheet at the front desk; salaried staff only record time off.
- On-call: one tech per week is on call. $150 for the week plus overtime for hours worked after hours.

## Steps (every two weeks)

1. Monday after the pay period ends: collect the two weeks of field timesheets. Chase missing ones (usually 3 or 4).
2. Check each sheet: add up daily hours, check lunch, check overtime over 40 hours, compare on-call hours with the after-hours calls in the dispatch spreadsheet.
3. Type hours for each person into ADP RUN: regular, overtime, on-call, PTO.
4. Fix questions with Sam, Ben or the employee (unreadable times, missing end times, weekend calls not written down). Usually about 6 corrections a payroll.
5. Preview payroll in ADP RUN, compare with last period, submit by Tuesday 5 pm.
6. File the timesheets in the binder for the year.

## Effort

Ruth spends about 5 hours per payroll on steps 1 to 5, more in January and July when overtime is high. 26 payrolls a year.

## Example

Pay period ending 2026-10-17 (PAY-2026-21). Field hours 1,912 regular, 188 overtime. Tech Frank Delgado wrote 52 hours but his days added up to 49.5; Ruth checked with Sam, who confirmed 49.5. Submitted 2026-10-20 at 3:40 pm.

## Pay rules to remember

- Overtime is time and a half over 40 hours in the week (Sunday to Saturday). Holidays do not count toward the 40.

- Apprentices are paid hourly at their step rate; their step changes on the anniversary date in the personnel file.

- PTO requests are on paper slips approved by Sam, Ben or Kyle. Ruth keeps the PTO balances in a small Excel sheet, because the balances in ADP RUN were never set up.

- New hires: Ruth enters them in ADP RUN from the paper new-hire packet and the I-9 and W-4 in the personnel file.

## When things go wrong

- Timesheet missing on Tuesday: pay 40 hours and correct next payroll.
- Overtime approved verbally by Kyle: Kyle initials the sheet.
- Ruth out: payroll is late unless she does it from home.
