# Installations (Residential Replacement and RTU)

Owner: Ben Okonkwo (Install Manager). Crew leads: Ray Mendez (crew 1), Owen Fisk (crew 2). Last updated February 2024.

## Purpose

Get sold systems installed on the promised day, to code, with permits, photos and warranty registration done, and the job ready to invoice.

## Volumes

310 residential installs and 40 commercial RTU replacements in the reporting year. Two crews, usually one residential install per crew per day; RTUs need a crane and are scheduled with Wes.

## Steps

1. Sale: Nadia's signed proposal and deposit go into a paper install folder. Ben logs it in "Install Log 2026.xlsx" (JOB number, customer, equipment, install date).

2. Equipment: Ben gives the equipment list to Lisa, who orders from Northgate. Ray picks up the equipment at the Northgate counter the morning of the install, about 40 minutes each way from our shop with loading.

3. Permit: Ben pulls the permit online and prints it for the folder.

4. Install day: crew lead checks the folder, does the install, starts up the system and records readings on the start-up sheet.

5. Photos: before leaving the job, the crew lead photographs the old equipment, the new equipment, and the data plate (model and serial number) of each new unit, and texts the photos to Ben. Ben prints them for the folder. No job is closed without data plate photos.

6. Walk-through: Ben or the crew lead walks the homeowner through the thermostat and filter. Customer signs the completion form.

7. Warranty registration: Ben registers each new unit on the Aerovance dealer portal using the serial numbers from the data plate photos. Aerovance gives a 10-year parts warranty if the unit is registered within 60 days of install; otherwise the base warranty is 5 years.

8. Close: Ben marks the job Done in the install log and gives the folder to Dana to invoice the balance.

## Example

JOB-2026-0907, Martin and Gail Ostrowski, 77 Bellweather Court, Gahanna. Aerovance 96% furnace and 3-ton AC, $12,450, installed 2026-09-15 by crew 1. Equipment picked up by Ray at 7:05 am. Registered on the portal 2026-09-29. Balance invoiced 2026-09-17 as INV-2026-1902.

## When things go wrong

- Equipment not at Northgate in the morning: Ray calls Lisa, crew does prep work while Lisa finds the unit at another branch.
- Inspection fails: Ben books the fix and the reinspection.
- Callback within 30 days: Sam or the crew lead goes back at no charge.
- Warranty claim on a registered unit: Ben files it on the portal with the serial number and the failed part goes back to Northgate.
