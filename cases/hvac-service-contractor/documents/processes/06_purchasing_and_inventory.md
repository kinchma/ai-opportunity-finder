# Purchasing and Inventory

Owner: Lisa Tran (Purchasing and Inventory Coordinator). Approver: Kyle Vossberg. Last updated June 2020.

## Purpose

Buy equipment and parts at the right price, have common parts on the shelf or on the vans, and keep track of what we spend.

## Suppliers

- Northgate Supply: Aerovance equipment, coils, motors, controls, most parts. Our main supplier since 1994. About $1.2 million of equipment a year plus parts. Pricing is our Northgate dealer price sheet. We have not asked anyone else to quote equipment since we became an Aerovance dealer.
- Ridgeview HVAC Supply: filters, some parts when Northgate is out. Ridgeview became an Aerovance distributor in 2025. In March 2026 their rep left a written quote for the same Aerovance models at 7.5% below our Northgate prices (in the purchasing binder, see data/supplier_quotes.csv). Kyle has it to review.
- Buckeye Sheet Metal: custom ductwork for installs.

## Purchase orders

1. Lisa writes a PO in the paper PO book (PO-2026-xxxx) with supplier, items, quantities, prices, and the job number if it is for a job.
2. Any PO over $500 needs Kyle's signature before it is placed. Kyle is often in the field, so Lisa texts him a photo of the PO and places the order when he replies OK, then gets the signature later.
3. Lisa places the order on the Northgate online account or by phone. Orders placed by 2 pm are delivered free the next morning.
4. Lisa receives deliveries at the warehouse door, checks against the PO, and files the packing slip with the PO for Dana.

## Warehouse parts cage

Common parts (capacitors, contactors, motors, igniters, filters, refrigerant) are kept in the cage. Lisa counts the cage the last Friday of each month in "Cage Count.xlsx" and reorders below the minimums.

## Van stock

Each service van carries a standard stock of about 120 common parts. Techs restock from the cage on Monday mornings and write what they took on the clipboard by the cage. Van stock is not counted. When a tech does not have a part on the van, he drives to the cage or to the Northgate counter. Techs say this happens about 2 times a week each.

## Example

PO-2026-0412, 2026-04-08, Northgate, 6 Aerovance 3-ton condensers and 6 coils for JOB-2026-0301 to 0306, $19,860. Texted to Kyle 9:15 am, OK at 1:40 pm, ordered 1:52 pm, delivered 2026-04-09.

## When things go wrong

- Kyle does not answer before 2 pm: order goes the next day, delivery a day later.
- Wrong part delivered: call Northgate for a swap on the next delivery.
- Part out at Northgate: try Ridgeview or another Northgate branch.
