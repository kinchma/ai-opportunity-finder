# Month-End Close

Owner: Dana Pruitt. Reviewer: Ruth Vossberg. Last updated November 2023.

## Purpose

Close each month in QuickBooks and produce the Monthly Numbers report for Glen, Ruth and Kyle.

## Timing

Target: report on Glen's desk by the 10th business day of the next month. Lately it has been closer to the 12th.

## Checklist (see data/month_end_checklist.csv)

1. Make sure all yellow service tickets for the month are entered (see Service Ticket to Invoice).

2. Make sure all install folders marked Done in the install log are invoiced.

3. Enter all supplier bills from the PO packing slips; match the Northgate statement.

4. Reconcile the two bank accounts and the credit card in QuickBooks.

5. Record Comfort Club renewals charged in the month (card processor report).

6. Record payroll journal from the ADP RUN reports.

7. Run QuickBooks profit and loss and AR aging.

8. Build the Monthly Numbers workbook:

   - Calls per tech and calls by type: copy from "Dispatch 2026.xlsx" and count by status Done.

   - Installs and install revenue: copy from Ben's "Install Log 2026.xlsx".

   - Commercial agreements active, renewed and lost: copy from Wes's "Commercial Agreements.xlsx".

   - Comfort Club members: from the Comfort Club tab.

   - Revenue by type: re-type the totals from the QuickBooks P&L into the workbook.

   - Web form count: count the emails in service@.

9. Ruth reviews and initials the checklist. Print the workbook for Glen.

## Effort

Dana estimates the copying, counting and re-typing in step 8 takes about 10 hours a month, because the three spreadsheets are laid out differently and the dispatch spreadsheet has duplicates to remove first. Steps 1 to 7 are another 12 to 15 hours.

## Example

September 2026 close: last yellow tickets came in 2026-10-06; bank reconciliations done 2026-10-09; Monthly Numbers printed 2026-10-16 (12th business day). September showed 512 service calls, 31 installs, 2 commercial agreements lost.

## Who uses the report

Glen looks at revenue by type and the install count. Kyle looks at calls per tech and callbacks. Ruth looks at AR aging and the payroll percentage. Wes gets the commercial page by email. Nobody else gets the workbook; Kyle has asked for a weekly version during the summer but there has not been time to build it.

## When things go wrong

- Install log and QuickBooks disagree on the install count: go through the folders with Ben.
- Northgate statement does not match the bills: call Northgate AR with the PO numbers.
- Dana out sick at month-end: the close waits; Ruth can enter bills but does not do the reconciliations.
