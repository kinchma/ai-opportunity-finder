# Scheduling and Dispatch

Owner: Trevor Haskins (Dispatcher). Backup: Marisol Quintero. Last updated January 2022.

## Purpose

Put every service call, Comfort Club tune-up and commercial PM visit on a tech's day, keep customers informed, and move jobs around when the day changes.

## Tools

- The whiteboard in the dispatch room: 14 techs across the top, two-hour windows down the side, a magnet with the job number for each call. Red magnets for P1 calls.
- "Dispatch 2026.xlsx": one row per call with status (New, Scheduled, Dispatched, Done, Needs Return, Parts on Order).
- Tech cell phones.
- Housecall Pro for reminder texts (see below).

## Daily steps

1. By 7:00 am, check the spreadsheet for new rows from the day before and the morning after-hours messages from Marisol.
2. Build the day on the whiteboard: P1 calls first, then booked calls, then tune-ups and PM visits. Match techs by skill (Sam, Derek and Hector for commercial; apprentices ride with Sam).
3. Copy the tech name and window from the board back into the spreadsheet row.
4. Text each tech their first two jobs. The rest are called out as they finish.
5. When a tech calls in "done", mark Done on the spreadsheet, wipe the magnet, give the next job by phone.
6. Late in the afternoon, Kyle copies tomorrow's calls from the spreadsheet into Housecall Pro so the reminder texts go out to customers the night before.

## Volumes

A normal day is 20 to 30 service calls plus tune-ups in season (spring and fall) and 1 to 2 commercial PM visits. Each tech calls in after every job, so Trevor takes 60 to 80 tech calls a day on top of customer calls. Customers calling to ask "when will the tech get here" are about 30 to 40 calls a day in summer and winter; the CSR walks to the board to see where the tech is.

## Example

2026-07-14: 31 calls on the board. Tech Ivan Petrov ran long at call SC-2026-3290 (compressor replacement). Trevor moved two of his afternoon calls to Grant Fuller, changed the magnets, updated the spreadsheet rows and called both customers. One customer had already received the Housecall Pro reminder with Ivan's name and called to ask why it changed.

## When things go wrong

- If the spreadsheet and board do not match, the board wins. Fix the spreadsheet at the end of the day.
- If Trevor is out, Marisol runs dispatch; CSR calls then roll to Jolene only.
- If a tech needs a part, mark "Parts on Order" and tell Lisa.
- If the board gets wiped by mistake, rebuild from the spreadsheet and the texts.
