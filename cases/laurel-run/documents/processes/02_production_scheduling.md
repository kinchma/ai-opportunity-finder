# Production scheduling

**Laurel Run Trailer Works, Inc. — fictional procedure, as of 2026-10-31**

Jonah Sutter, Production Scheduler, keeps the Excel schedule and the shop whiteboard. Owen Bellamy, Plant Manager, owns the floor. The schedule, order register and inventory sheet are separate; the paper work order travels through the shop.

## Weekly setup

1. Priya Ellwood, Dealer Account Coordinator, receives the dealer's written acceptance of an approved quote and enters the order in the Excel order register. She copies the approved model and options into the register and the paper build packet. An unaccepted quote is still an offer, not a confirmed order to schedule.
2. Jonah checks the approved orders, stock notes and input from Theo Lowry, Fabrication Supervisor, and Avery Carver, Assembly Supervisor. He assigns a separate `WO-YYYY-NNNN` to each trailer, even if one dealer order has several trailers, and puts a target date against the work. Order and work-order numbers are different sequences; neither is a VIN.
3. Each week Jonah sequences the confirmed work orders in his Excel schedule and on the whiteboard, then gives the paper packets to the floor. They go through fabrication, paint, assembly and inspection. Theo and Avery report progress and material exceptions back to Jonah, who updates the Excel schedule and the board as the order of work changes.

The shop completes about 30 trailers per working week over 50 working weeks, or 1,500 in the reporting year. The unit log for 2025-11-01 through 2026-10-31 shows 125 completions and 125 shipments each month. October's completions were 50 Trailfield, 45 Enclosure and 30 Workspan. These are not a promise for a particular week.

## Changes, shortages and dates

If a dealer wants a specification change, Tessa Bellamy, Dealer Sales Manager, obtains the dealer's approval. Jonah issues a dated replacement packet page for the approved revision; Theo and Avery return the old page. Iris Calder, Quality and Warranty Lead, checks the current revision at inspection. When an older copy is in hand, check it against the changed page rather than building from it. There are 24 examples in a year where a changed page needs verification against an older copy. Do not treat a proposed change as approved just because it has been discussed.

If material is short, Theo or Avery tells Jonah. Check stock notes and expected receipts with Devon Kersey, Materials and Purchasing Manager, and adjust the sequence; useful alternative work may be assigned. In four separate shortage events that year, three workers were held for three hours each before reassignment. No substitution or safety compromise was recorded; these were not whole-plant stoppages. Do not substitute material without Felix Harrow, Manufacturing Engineer, and Iris confirming fit and required specifications. Brakes, tires, couplers, lighting and substitutions need fit and applicable compliance review. A component rating alone does not establish trailer payload; use the completed unit's certification and load labels.

Jonah tells Priya when a promised date moves, so she can update the dealer. Dates are estimates, not guarantees. Priya handles around 300 dealer order-status follow-ups in a year, checking with Jonah or Mateo Renshaw, Shipping Coordinator, for current status; these are separate from warranty and stockout inquiries. If an urgent hazard is reported, the supervisor tells Owen immediately and unsafe equipment is taken out of service. The company records do not name a separate backup scheduler.

## Example and records to keep

Cedar Bend Trailer Sales, `DLR-0001`, requested `QTE-2026-1180` on 2026-10-14 for one Trailfield with a spare-tire **mount only**. Priya prepared the quote, Tessa approved the $4,920 dealer total, and the dealer accepted on 2026-10-15. The resulting `ORD-2026-1120` had `WO-2026-1240` dated 2026-10-15; Jonah scheduled it. Finish inspection was 2026-10-26 and shipment was 2026-10-27. The unit is included in October's counts; no revision is shown in this sample.

Keep the written dealer approval with the email, the order in the register, the schedule in Excel and the current dated page in the paper packet. Iris keeps the final-inspection checklist with the packet. Mateo schedules shipment; after shipping confirmation, Elena Corwin, Accounting Clerk, issues the dealer invoice at shipment. A scheduled date or an accepted order is not a shipment.
