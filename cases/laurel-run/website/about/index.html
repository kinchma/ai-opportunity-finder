About Laurel Run

Fictional demonstration: Laurel Run Trailer Works, its people, dealers, and products are invented. No purchases or messages are processed.

A family story, built around useful work

Elwood Bellamy founded Laurel Run Trailer Works in 1988. He built the business around practical trailers and a relationship with independent dealers. He retired in 2018, and the Bellamy family continues to own the company.

Today, Mara Bellamy is president and CEO, Owen Bellamy is plant manager, and Tessa Bellamy manages dealer sales. The fictional business has 50 employees and works with approximately 80 independent dealers. Dealers set their own retail prices; Laurel Run does not sell factory-direct to retail customers.

One Pennsylvania facility

Laurel Run operates its facility in Hollow Glen, an invented Pennsylvania community. The site brings cutting, welding, paint, final assembly, inspection, inventory, and administration together in one place. The fictional lineup grew to include enclosed cargo trailers in the 2000s and equipment trailers in the 2010s, alongside open utility trailers.

Choosing a trailer starts with knowing the work it must do. Dealers can help discuss configurations, current availability, and the labels on a finished unit.
