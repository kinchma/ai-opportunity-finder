A wider deck for equipment

The Workspan brings an 82-inch-wide by 18-foot open steel deck together with loading ramps and a tandem-axle arrangement. The two axles are each rated at 5,200 pounds and have electric brakes. It is designed as a starting point for equipment-hauling conversations, where the complete load, its dimensions, and the towing combination all matter.

Each axle's rating describes that component; adding axle ratings does not establish this trailer's GVWR or payload. Check the completed trailer's certification and load labels and the limits of the tow vehicle, hitch, and other components. The weight of the trailer and its installed equipment affects available payload.

Think through loading and securement

Discuss the equipment's weight, dimensions, ground clearance, and loading requirements with a dealer. Ask about ramp, tie-down, and coupler choices for the particular configuration, and verify that all equipment and attachment points are appropriate for the intended load. Follow the finished trailer's instructions and applicable securement rules before travel.
