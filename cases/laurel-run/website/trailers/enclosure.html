Room for the things you bring along

The Enclosure is a 7-foot by 14-foot enclosed cargo trailer for tools, supplies, and other materials. A steel frame supports an aluminum exterior and a plywood-lined interior. Its tandem-axle layout, rear door, and electric-braked axles make it a useful starting point when an enclosed space is important to the job.

Nominal exterior dimensions do not establish the clear interior dimensions, door opening, or usable load. These can vary with the finished configuration. Check the certification and load labels on the completed unit, and confirm that the tow vehicle, hitch, and brake system are suited to the combination.

Make the space work for you

Before choosing a configuration, consider the size and weight of your cargo, the clearances needed to load it, and how items will be restrained. A dealer can discuss current colors and available options, along with the dimensions and specifications of a particular trailer. Keep doors secured for travel and follow the instructions supplied with the finished unit.
