Welcome to
Laurel Run Trailer Works

From our shop in Hollow Glen, Pennsylvania, we build utility, enclosed cargo, and equipment trailers for work on the road and at the job site. Our family started Laurel Run in 1988 with a simple idea: make useful equipment and stand behind the people who use it.

Today our team cuts, welds, paints, assembles, and checks trailers for a network of independent dealers. Each dealer can help you choose a model, discuss options, and arrange a purchase.
