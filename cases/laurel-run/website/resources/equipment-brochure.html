Workspan: a wider deck

With a nominal 82-inch-wide by 18-foot open steel deck, rear ramps, and tandem electric-braked axles, Workspan starts a conversation about carrying equipment. Each axle is rated at 5,200 pounds, which is a component rating and not the trailer's gross weight or payload rating.

Before choosing a configuration

Consider equipment dimensions, weight, ground clearance, loading ramps, tie-downs, hitch, and tow-vehicle ratings together. Refer to the finished trailer's certification and load labels and the applicable instructions; do not infer payload by adding axle ratings.

Independent dealer support

Independent dealers can discuss configuration, availability, and retail pricing. Laurel Run does not sell factory-direct to retail customers. This is a fictional overview, not an offer for an actual trailer. Use your browser's print command to keep a copy.
