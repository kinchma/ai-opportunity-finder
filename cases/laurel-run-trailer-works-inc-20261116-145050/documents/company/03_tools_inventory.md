# Tools and records by function

**As of 2026-10-31.** Laurel Run, its people, its use of these tools, the records and its procedures are fictional. Named commercial software is real. The use described here is a case assumption; it is not a claim that the software's editions have features beyond those stated. A named owner is the business owner of the records, not necessarily a software administrator. Files on the shared office drive are maintained by their listed owners. Printed forms and email remain part of the record.
