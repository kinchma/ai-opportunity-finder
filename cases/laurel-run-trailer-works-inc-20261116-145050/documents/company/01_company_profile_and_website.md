# Laurel Run Trailer Works, Inc. — company profile

**Fictional case, as of October 31, 2026.** Laurel Run, every person and every dealer, supplier, product and company figure in this case are invented for academic use. Hollow Glen is an invented Pennsylvania community, not a real street address. This is not a profile of any actual manufacturer. The operating accounts below are deliberately constructed assumptions, not observations, audited figures, or forecasts. Software names and the linked research sources are real.

Laurel Run is a family-owned, Pennsylvania-based builder of light open utility, enclosed cargo and equipment trailers. The company sells to approximately 80 independent dealers, not directly to retail customers; dealers set their own retail prices. As of the snapshot it employs exactly 50 people. One factory in fictional Hollow Glen houses cutting, welding, paint, final assembly, inspection, inventory and administration. Incoming freight is handled at the receiving bay; dealers arrange or accept delivery by appointment.

Elwood Bellamy, a fictional fabricator, founded the business in 1988 and retired in 2018. His children Mara, Owen and Tessa Bellamy work in the business: Mara is president and CEO, Owen is plant manager, and Tessa manages dealer sales. The Bellamy family owns the company. Elwood is not an employee and is not included in the headcount. The company added enclosed cargo trailers in the 2000s and the equipment family in the 2010s. These milestones are invented.

## TTM economics: November 1, 2025–October 31, 2026

Recognized revenue is wholesale dealer revenue for trailers shipped, including separately priced factory options, plus replacement parts sold to dealers. Sales are recognized at shipment, when the fictional standard sales terms transfer control to the dealer. Amounts collected as sales tax are excluded; the company does not separately bill freight in this illustrative year. Dealers' retail sales are not Laurel Run revenue. The shipped and completed quantities are both 1,500 because the fictional beginning and ending finished-trailer counts are the same.

**Research basis, not evidence of Laurel Run's actual results:** The [2022 Census SUSB size data](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/susb/tables/2022/us_state_naics_detailedsizes_2022.txt), summarized in `../research/02_economics.md`, imply about $255,216 receipts per employee for enterprises of 20–49 in the broader travel-trailer-and-camper category. That is a noisy 2022 aggregate ratio, not a 2026, 50-person, non-RV-company average. The 15% gross and 3% operating margins are selected assumptions informed by imperfect larger-company RV and commercial-trailer comparisons, not measured small-utility-trailer benchmarks; classification, cycle and cost allocation differ. The [BLS occupational data](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/naics4_336200.htm) support the plausibility of a production-heavy structure, not this exact roster. # Website copy — About Laurel Run

## Built for the work ahead

Laurel Run Trailer Works builds trailers for people who need dependable equipment to get to the next job. Our family started the shop in Pennsylvania in 1988. Today our team cuts, welds, paints, assembles and checks utility, enclosed cargo and equipment trailers at our Hollow Glen facility.

We work through independent dealers. Your local dealer can help you choose a configuration, discuss available options, provide the retail price, and handle delivery and warranty questions. Trailer specifications, availability and dealer pricing can change; ask a dealer for the configuration and capacity label that apply to the unit you are considering.

# Website copy — Trailfield open utility trailers

## A straightforward open hauler

The Trailfield 6×12 is our baseline single-axle utility trailer, with a steel frame, open deck, electric-braked spring axle, lights and rear loading gate. It is suited to general-purpose equipment and materials when matched to the required load and tow vehicle. Ask your dealer about options such as a spare-tire mount or upgraded jack. The fictional standard dealer base is $4,800; dealers set their own retail prices. Check the finished unit's certification and load labels rather than assuming payload from nominal dimensions.

# Website copy — Enclosure cargo trailers

## Covered space for tools and materials

The Enclosure 7×14 is our baseline tandem-axle enclosed cargo trailer, with a steel frame, aluminum outer panels, plywood interior lining, rear door, lights and electric-braked axles. Dealers can quote selected options and confirm current colors and availability. The fictional standard dealer base is $9,600 before options; dealers set retail prices. Interior dimensions and usable load depend on configuration; check the unit labels.

# Website copy — Workspan equipment trailers

## A wider deck for equipment

The Workspan baseline provides an 82-inch-wide, 18-foot deck and tandem spring axles with electric brakes, a steel frame, lighting and loading ramps. Ask a dealer to confirm ramp, tie-down and coupler choices for the intended load and tow vehicle. The fictional standard dealer base is $12,000 before options; dealers set retail prices. Final ratings and payload are on the completed trailer's labels.
