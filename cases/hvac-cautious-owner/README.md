# Larkmoor Comfort Heating & Air, Inc. (case folder)

Fictional company created for an academic test case. Snapshot date: 2026-10-31. Reporting year: 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31.

Larkmoor Comfort is a family-owned heating and air conditioning service and installation contractor based in the fictional town of Larkmoor, Ohio, serving the north and east Columbus suburbs. About 35 employees. Residential repair, replacement and a residential maintenance plan (the Comfort Club), plus maintenance agreements with about 90 commercial building owners and property managers.

## Files

- `website/index.html`: the public website.
- `documents/company/`: company profile, organization and roles, tools inventory, marketing.
- `documents/processes/`: eight internal process documents (01 to 08).
- `interviews/`: one transcript per process (01 to 08), plus a marketing and reviews interview (09); `interviews/personas/` has one short persona per function.
- `data/`: CSV samples and `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`.
- `research/`: external research notes (context only, not company facts).
