# Research Note 3 - Operations, Purchasing and Marketing

External context only, gathered October 2026.

## Truck time and supply house trips

An ACCA financial analysis cited by Simpro puts the cost of rolling an HVAC truck at about $84 per hour, and says an unplanned supply house trip uses roughly an hour of paid technician time with no revenue. Source: https://www.simprogroup.com/blog/how-to-run-a-successful-hvac-business

## Supplier pricing

Benchmark guides recommend reviewing supplier pricing and job costing regularly rather than once a year. Source: https://www.fieldpulse.com/resources/blog/hvac-performance-metrics

Equipment is usually bought through regional distributors that carry one or a few manufacturer brands; a contractor's dealer price depends on volume and negotiation. This is general knowledge of the trade, not a published benchmark.

## Paperwork in the office

ServiceTrade describes commercial contractor offices where job tickets pile up and staff re-enter handwritten forms into spreadsheets in the evening. Source: https://servicetrade.com/resources/blog/paper-chaos-to-digital-data-clarity/

## Marketing

A ServiceTitan market report cited by Webtonic says word-of-mouth referrals make up about 71% of residential contractor business volume. Source: https://www.webtonic.io/blog/hvac-lifecycle-and-retention-marketing-statistics/

## Limits

Mostly vendor content. Use for direction, not as precise targets.
