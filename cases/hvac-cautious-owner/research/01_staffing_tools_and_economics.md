# Research Note 1 - Staffing, Tools and Economics of Small HVAC Contractors

External context only, gathered October 2026. These are published industry figures from vendor blogs and trade press, not facts about any one company. Vendor sources sell software and may lean optimistic.

## Revenue per technician and margins

A 2026 contractor benchmark roundup puts revenue per service technician at roughly $150,000 to $250,000 a year and industry-average net margin at 5% to 12%, with top-quartile firms at 15% to 25%. Source: https://simprogroup.com/blog/hvac-industry-statistics-2026

Other vendor guides put the average net margin around 8% to 12% and a healthy gross margin near 50% to 55%. Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/learn/guide-to-hvac-profits-salaries-revenue/

## Tools in use

A Verizon Connect survey cited by BuildOps found about 51% of field service companies use field service management software, about 25% still use spreadsheets, and the rest rely on whiteboards and other manual methods. Source: https://buildops.com/manual-processes-to-field-service-management-software

A 2026 Salesforce field service survey of about 2,300 professionals found 52% still use spreadsheets and 43% still rely on paper logs for field assets. Source: https://www.salesforce.com/service/resources/state-of-field-service-report/

## Limits

Most figures come from software vendors and mix company sizes. Small family firms with 20 to 50 staff are often not separated out. Treat the ranges as rough context.
