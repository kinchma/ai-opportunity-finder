# Tools Inventory

Updated October 2026 by Dana Pruitt. What we use, who owns it, and how.

## Accounting and finance

- QuickBooks Desktop Premier (owner: Dana). Customers, invoices, bills, payments, bank reconciliation. All service tickets and install jobs are typed in by Dana from the paper ticket or the install folder. One license plus a read-only login for Ruth.
- Excel month-end workbook "Monthly Numbers 2026.xlsx" (owner: Dana). Revenue by type, calls per tech, install count, Comfort Club count. Built each month from the dispatch spreadsheet, Wes's agreement sheet, Ben's install log and QuickBooks reports.

## Service and dispatch

- Paper service tickets, three-part carbon (owner: Trevor orders them). Tech writes work done, parts used, hours, and gets the customer signature. White copy to the customer, yellow to the office, pink stays in the van book.

- Excel dispatch spreadsheet "Dispatch 2026.xlsx" on the shared drive (owner: Trevor). One row per call: date, customer, address, problem, tech, status.

- Whiteboard in the dispatch room (owner: Trevor). Techs across the top, time slots down the side, magnets for jobs.

- Company cell phones for every tech. Techs call or text the office when they finish a job.

- Housecall Pro (owner: Kyle). Bought in March 2024 for scheduling and mobile tickets, $299 per month. Today it is used only to send appointment reminder texts; the techs never moved to it. Kyle copies the next day's calls into it each evening so the texts go out.

## Commercial

- Excel "Commercial Agreements.xlsx" (owner: Wes). One row per agreement: customer, buildings, visits per year, price, start and end dates, renewal notes.
- Excel "Commercial Pricing 2026.xlsx" on Wes's laptop (owner: Wes). Labor rates, markup by part category, RTU pricing by tonnage.

## Installs and sales

- Paper install folders (owner: Ben). Proposal, equipment order, permit, photos printed or on the installer's phone, final walk-through checklist, warranty registration sheet.
- Excel "Install Log 2026.xlsx" (owner: Ben).
- Proposal template in Word and a financing partner web portal (owner: Nadia).
- Aerovance dealer portal (owner: Ben, Nadia also has a login). Product registration for warranties is done here.

## Purchasing and inventory

- Northgate Supply online ordering account (owner: Lisa). Next-morning delivery is free for orders placed by 2 pm.
- Purchase order book, paper, numbered PO-2026-xxxx (owner: Lisa). Kyle signs any PO over $500.
- Excel "Cage Count.xlsx" for the warehouse parts cage (owner: Lisa). Van stock is not tracked.

## Payroll and HR

- ADP RUN (owner: Ruth). Biweekly payroll for 35 people.
- Paper weekly timesheets for field staff. Office staff use a sign-in sheet.
- Paper personnel files in Ruth's office.

## Phones, email and other

- Desk phones with a basic hunt group (owner: Dana as the contact for the phone vendor).
- Outlook email: service@, office@, and personal addresses. Website form messages arrive in service@.
- Midstate Answering: after-hours calls. Messages come by email to service@ and the on-call tech gets a text for no-heat and no-cool calls.
- Google Business Profile and a Facebook page (owner: Jolene).
