# Larkmoor Comfort Heating & Air - Company Profile

Prepared by Dana Pruitt for the owners, updated October 2026. Reporting year: November 1, 2025 to October 31, 2026.

## History

Glen Vossberg started Larkmoor Comfort in 1994 after twelve years as a service technician for another contractor. He worked out of his garage with one van. His wife Ruth Vossberg ran the books and the phone from the kitchen table. The company moved to its current building at 2290 Fenwick Road, Larkmoor, Ohio in 2003. The building has the office, a small warehouse with a parts cage, and parking for the fleet.

Their son Kyle Vossberg joined in 2011 as a service technician and became operations manager in 2019. Glen is still president and still signs every equipment purchase over $10,000 and every commercial contract. Ruth is vice president and handles payroll and HR.

## What we do

Residential service and repair: furnaces, heat pumps, central air conditioners, ductless mini-splits, humidifiers, thermostats. Standard service call (diagnostic) fee is $99. Average residential repair ticket is about $410.

Residential system replacement: a Comfort Advisor (Nadia Ferris) visits, measures and gives a written proposal with three options. Typical installed price is $9,500 to $16,000 for a furnace and air conditioner. Average install ticket is about $11,800. We sell mostly Aerovance equipment, bought through Northgate Supply.

Comfort Club maintenance plan: $189 per year, two tune-ups (spring cooling, fall heating), priority scheduling, no overtime charge, 15% off repairs. 640 members at the snapshot.

Commercial: preventive maintenance (PM) agreements with about 90 building owners and property managers (offices, retail strips, churches, two private schools). Agreements are one year, usually 2 to 4 visits per year. Commercial PM revenue is about $410,000 a year. We also replace rooftop units (RTUs), about 40 per year.

## Volumes for the reporting year

- Service calls (residential and commercial repair): about 5,200
- Comfort Club tune-ups scheduled: 1,280 (640 members x 2)
- Residential installs: 310
- Commercial RTU replacements: 40
- Commercial PM visits: about 290 across 90 agreements
- After-hours messages from the answering service: about 1,050
- Website request forms: about 410

## Revenue and size

Revenue for the reporting year is about $6.4 million: residential service and repair about $2.1 million, residential replacement about $3.4 million (310 installs at about $11,800 average is $3.66 million gross, less some financing fees and adjustments), commercial about $0.8 million, Comfort Club about $121,000. Equipment purchases from Northgate Supply are about $1.2 million a year.

We have 35 employees: 11 in the office and 24 in the field (14 service technicians, 8 installers, 2 apprentices). We run 16 service vans and 3 install trucks.

## Customers and area

Mostly homeowners in Larkmoor, Westerville, Gahanna, New Albany, Delaware and the north and east Columbus suburbs. About 70% of residential customers have used us before. Commercial customers are mostly local property managers; the largest is Brookfield Pike Properties with 14 buildings under agreement.

## Main suppliers

- Northgate Supply (Columbus branch): Aerovance equipment, coils, most parts. We have bought from them since 1994.
- Ridgeview HVAC Supply: filters and some parts when Northgate is out.
- Buckeye Sheet Metal: custom ductwork and transitions.
- Fleet fuel cards, uniforms and the answering service (Midstate Answering) are the other regular vendors.
