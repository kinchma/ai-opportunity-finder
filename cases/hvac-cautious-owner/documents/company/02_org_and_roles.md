## How work is shared

Several people cover more than one job. Marisol answers calls and covers dispatch, so she already knows which customers are waiting. Lisa buys parts and also keeps the parts cage counts. Ruth does payroll and HR paperwork from the same employee files. Sam runs a van and trains the new techs on the jobs he is already running.

Dana does the books and is also the person everyone calls about computer and phone problems. Ray leads an install crew and also drives to Northgate to pick up equipment. Wes sells commercial agreements, prices commercial jobs and tracks renewals, and is the only one who works on the commercial pricing sheet. Kyle approves every purchase order over $500 on top of running operations, so orders wait when he is in the field.
