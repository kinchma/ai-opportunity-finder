# Maintenance Agreements (Commercial PM and Comfort Club)

Owners: Wes Delacroix (commercial), Jolene Fairweather (Comfort Club). Last updated May 2022.

## Commercial PM agreements

We have about 90 commercial preventive maintenance agreements, mostly one year, with 2 to 4 visits per year (about 290 visits a year). Average agreement is about $4,550 a year; together about $410,000. Agreements are listed in "Commercial Agreements.xlsx" with start and end dates.

Pricing: Wes prices new agreements and commercial repair and RTU quotes from "Commercial Pricing 2026.xlsx" (labor rates by visit type, markup by part category, RTU prices by tonnage). Glen signs agreements over $10,000.

## Renewal procedure

1. On the first business day of each month, Wes filters the agreement sheet for end dates 90 days out.
2. Wes sends a renewal letter and the new price to each of those customers 90 days before the end date.
3. Follow-up call at 60 days and again at 30 days if not signed.
4. Signed renewals: update the end date in the sheet and give the visit months to Trevor.
5. If a customer does not renew, mark it "Lost" with the reason.

## Commercial PM visit steps

1. Trevor books the visits for the month from Wes's list.
2. Tech does the checklist for each unit (filters, belts, coils, refrigerant, electrical) on the paper PM form.
3. Repairs found during PM get a separate quote from Wes.

## Comfort Club

640 residential members at $189 a year, two tune-ups each: 1,280 tune-ups a year. Members sign up with the tech or Nadia, and renew automatically on their card each year.

Booking steps:
1. In March (cooling) and September (heating), Jolene prints the member list from the Comfort Club tab.
2. Jolene and Marisol call each member to book a tune-up. Leave a message if no answer and try again later in the week. It usually takes 2 to 3 calls to book one member.
3. Booked tune-ups go into the dispatch spreadsheet like any call.
4. Members not reached by the end of the season are skipped until the next season.

## Example

Comfort Club member CC-0418, Linda Garvey, fall tune-up booked on the third call, 2026-09-22, done 2026-10-06.

## When things go wrong

- A commercial customer calls about a missed visit: check the agreement end date first; if it lapsed, Wes calls them.
- A member says they never got a tune-up: check the call log notes in the Comfort Club tab and book them first.
