Order entry from email, EDI gaps and phone calls is a major labor cost for distributors. Extracting line items, quantities and customer references from unstructured orders, then validating them against the catalog and customer pricing, reduces re-keying and shipping errors.

Customer service inquiries about order status, delivery dates, backorders and returns are repetitive. An assistant connected to the order and shipping systems can answer most of them and escalate exceptions.

Invoice and purchase-order matching is a high-volume accounting task for distributors dealing with many suppliers. Automatically matching documents and routing mismatches for review shortens the payables cycle and catches billing errors.

Inventory and replenishment suffer when demand data is poor. Before forecasting automation, clean the item master and unit-of-measure data, since duplicate or inconsistent item records quietly undermine every downstream tool.

Supplier communication, such as chasing late shipments and confirming acknowledgments, follows a predictable pattern that can be drafted automatically for a buyer to review and send.
