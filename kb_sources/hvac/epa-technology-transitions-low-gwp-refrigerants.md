Under the AIM Act, EPA's 2023 Technology Transitions Rule limits the global warming potential (GWP) of refrigerants in new equipment. For residential and light commercial air conditioning and heat pump systems the limit is 700 GWP: products using higher-GWP refrigerants could not be manufactured or imported from January 1, 2025, and the 2023 rule also set January 1, 2026 as the last date to install new split systems built with the older refrigerants. Most replacement equipment now uses lower-GWP refrigerants, many of them mildly flammable (A2L), which brings new handling, training and code requirements.

On May 21, 2026, EPA finalized a reconsideration of the 2023 rule. For residential and light commercial systems it removed the January 1, 2026 installation deadline for equipment whose components were manufactured or imported before January 1, 2025, so that existing inventory can be installed until it runs out. The underlying 700 GWP limit for new manufacture was not changed.

What this means for operations: a contractor now carries two generations of equipment and refrigerant. Quotes, inventory and job records need to show which refrigerant each system uses, which units are older-refrigerant inventory, and whether technicians and vans are equipped for A2L work. Service of existing equipment continues for years, so tracking the refrigerant installed at each customer site supports planning, pricing and safety.
