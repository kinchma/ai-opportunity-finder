Client intake and conflict or engagement checks involve repeating the same questions and entering the same data into several systems. Structured intake forms combined with an agent that summarizes and routes new matters reduce administrative time.

Document review, drafting from precedents and summarizing long materials are the most common uses of AI in firms. Because work product carries professional liability, every output needs review by a qualified person, and confidential client data should stay within approved environments.

Time capture and billing are frequent sources of lost revenue. Drafting time entries from calendar and email activity, for the professional to approve, recovers unbilled time with low risk.

Knowledge search across past work, templates and internal guidance saves staff from recreating work. A retrieval assistant grounded in the firm's own documents, with citations to the source file, is usually safer than a general chatbot.

Client communication, such as status updates and deadline reminders, can be drafted automatically and approved by the responsible person before sending.
