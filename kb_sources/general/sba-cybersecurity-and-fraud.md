Small businesses are attractive targets because they hold customer and payment data but have small protection budgets. The SBA's baseline is: train employees on phishing, safe browsing, suspicious downloads and protecting vendor and customer data; secure the network with encryption and a firewall, hide the Wi-Fi name, password-protect the router and require a VPN for remote work; run antivirus on every computer with automatic updates for operating systems, browsers and applications.

Require multi-factor authentication (two or more of a password or PIN, a physical token or phone, or biometrics), especially on financial, accounting and payroll systems, and check that those vendors offer it. Monitor cloud and SaaS accounts.

Protect data by isolating payment systems from general browsing, locking devices, giving each employee a separate account with a strong password, limiting administrator rights, auditing access regularly, collecting devices from former employees, and backing up critical data (weekly to cloud storage is the SBA's suggestion) with periodic checks of cloud sharing permissions.

Common threats are malware, viruses, ransomware, spyware and phishing. Free assessment tools include the FCC Small Biz Cyber Planner, CISA's Cyber Resilience Review and vulnerability scans, and FTC resources; federal contractors should review CMMC requirements.

Fraud aimed at small businesses: phishing that impersonates the SBA or other agencies, grant or loan offers that promise approval for an upfront fee, identity theft using the EIN or SSN, and impostors posing as SBA representatives or disaster inspectors. Safeguards: official SBA email ends in @sba.gov, applying for SBA programs is free, never pay a middleman, check application numbers against your records and monitor business and personal credit reports.

Any AI tool that reads company email, documents or customer records inherits these rules: least privilege, separate accounts, MFA on the systems it connects to, and no sensitive data sent to services the business does not control.
