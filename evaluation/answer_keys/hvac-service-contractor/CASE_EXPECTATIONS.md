# Case Expectations - Larkmoor Comfort Heating & Air

Evaluator-only.

## Industry

From the website: residential and light commercial HVAC (heating and air conditioning) service, repair and installation contractor in the Columbus, Ohio suburbs, with a residential maintenance plan and commercial preventive maintenance agreements. Any phrase containing "HVAC" is correct.

## What discovery should and should not claim

- Should: family-owned since 1994, about 35 people, 14 service technicians and two install crews, Comfort Club at $189 a year with 600+ members, about 90 commercial buildings, Aerovance dealer, phone, email and a web request form, after-hours emergency service.
- Social presence: only Facebook is linked on the page. Must not claim Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube or X, and must not invent handles.
- Must not claim online booking, a chat widget, a customer portal, financing details beyond what the page says, or reviews counts (the page does not show them).

## A strong analysis should

- Put ticket capture (I01), commercial renewal tracking (I03) and Comfort Club tune-up booking (I06) at or near the top. Reasons: large recurring hours or revenue, clear data already in hand, low risk, quick to pilot.
- Notice the web lead and after-hours intake (I04, I05) and dispatch double entry (I02) as related intake and scheduling work.
- Detect both conflicts: renewal timing (C1) and data plate photos (C2).

## Non-AI issues it should not dress up as AI

- I09 supplier re-quote (the largest dollar item): a purchasing decision.
- I10 Housecall Pro: adopt or cancel.
- I11 counter pickups: switch to delivery.
- I12 pricing sheet on one laptop: share, document, cross-train.
- I14 warranty registration: checklist and job-close rule.

Flagging these as quick wins or management actions is good; proposing an AI agent for them is a scoring miss.

## Must not invent

Revenue other than about $6.4 million, staff names not in the files, software the company does not use (no ServiceTitan, no QuickBooks Online), customer counts or review counts beyond the files, and any outage, lawsuit or incident not in the material.
