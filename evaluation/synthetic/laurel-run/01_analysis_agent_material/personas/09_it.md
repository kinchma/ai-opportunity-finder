## Short interview

**Q: What do you keep on the IT side?**  
**A:** An Excel device and access list and vendor contact details. I coordinate the outside IT support contractor. (`company/03_tools_inventory.md`)

**Q: Can a new hire get all systems immediately?**  
**A:** No. Send me the approved paper request for the access the job needs. If approval is outstanding, that access stays pending. (`processes/09_onboarding.md`)

**Q: How should someone send you a routine maintenance request?**  
**A:** Use Outlook and include the location or equipment and the work needed. I track service on the maintenance list. (`processes/10_facilities_upkeep.md`)

**Q: What if it’s an urgent hazard?**  
**A:** Tell the supervisor immediately. The supervisor reports it to Owen, and unsafe equipment is taken out of service; routine service scheduling is not the immediate response. (`processes/10_facilities_upkeep.md`)
