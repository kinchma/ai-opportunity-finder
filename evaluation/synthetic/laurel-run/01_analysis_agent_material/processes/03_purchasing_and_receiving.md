# Purchasing and receiving

**Laurel Run Trailer Works, Inc. — fictional procedure, as of 2026-10-31**

Devon Kersey, Materials and Purchasing Manager, looks after purchasing and inventory. Jalen Sorrell is the Buyer, Cora Whitlock is the Receiving and Inventory Clerk, and Elena Corwin is the Accounting Clerk. We use Excel for supplier, PO and inventory lists, Outlook to send POs, paper for receiving, and QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise for supplier bills. The stock sheet is the working count.

## Before ordering

Each week Cora hand-counts six selected frequently used SKU categories, notes the counts on paper and updates the Excel inventory sheet. Devon looks at the on-hand figures, open POs and likely near-term requirements and uses his experience to decide quantities. For example, on 2026-10-30 Cora counted 21 SKU-0001 axles, matching the sheet. That count is an example, not a complete inventory reconciliation.

Jalen prepares the PO for Devon to approve, including the quantity. After Devon approves the PO and quantity, Jalen assigns the next PO number and emails it to the supplier. Keep the supplier, item, quantity, delivered price and PO reference in the Excel PO list. PO numbers have their own sequence. Northmere Metals Service LLC (SUP-0003) has weekly releases for steel stock.

## At the receiving bay

Cora counts what comes in against the PO, records the receipt on paper and signs the packing slip. If the delivery is short or damaged, she tells Jalen **before updating the inventory sheet**. Record what actually arrived, not the whole order. The receipt and signed slip go with the PO and supplier bill for accounting; a partial delivery is a partial receipt.

Elena matches the supplier bill, signed packing slip and PO before entering the bill in QuickBooks. Check the item, quantities and price against the papers. Send a missing or mismatched item in the packet to Devon for exception approval before entry; don't treat an incomplete packet as a normal match. Devon approves exceptions. If Devon is away, don't assume another person has that approval: bring it back to Devon when available.

There are roughly 480 bill/receipt packets in a year. That includes partial deliveries and invoices, not 480 different purchase orders. Elena matches packets as bills and receipts come in.

## Axle release and supplier offers

Ridge Hollow Components LLC (SUP-0001) is the supplier of record for SKU-0001, the Trailfield 3,500-lb electric-braked spring axle. It includes both drums and hubs, has an 89-inch hub face, 74-inch spring center and five-on-4.5-inch bolt pattern; springs and hangers are separate. The regular release is 50 each monthly at $276 each delivered, excluding sales tax. The selected PO lines show 600 received in the reporting year, not all company purchases.

PO-2026-0237 was ordered 2026-10-08 and all 50 were received 2026-10-15. The line was $13,800 delivered, excluding sales tax, with net-30 terms. Ridge Hollow has supplied the comparable specification since June 2003; supplier notices changed prices over time. No formal competitive re-quote was solicited for this SKU before the unsolicited 2026 alternatives.

Keep the written offers in the supplier-price sample, separate from POs. Brackenway Parts Resale LLC (SUP-0002) offered $240 each on 2026-10-16; Willow Vale Trailer Supply LLC (SUP-0010) offered $250 each on 2026-10-19. Both are valid through 2026-11-15 and quote 600 annually in 50-unit monthly releases, net 30, delivered with freight included and sales tax excluded. The specification, packaged contents, freight, lot-size and stated lead-time basis match the current comparison; the lead-time duration is not specified. As of 2026-10-31 these are offers only: neither supplier has been switched to or purchased from for this item. Engineering fit/compliance review and supplier and financial due diligence are still needed before a switch.

If a shortage affects the floor, Theo Lowry or Avery Carver reports materials exceptions to Jonah Sutter for the schedule. Do not substitute material without Felix Harrow, Manufacturing Engineer, and Iris Calder, Quality and Warranty Lead, confirming fit and required specifications. Brakes, tires, couplers, lighting and substitutions need fit and applicable compliance review; an axle rating alone does not establish a trailer's payload. Check the finished trailer's certification and load labels.
