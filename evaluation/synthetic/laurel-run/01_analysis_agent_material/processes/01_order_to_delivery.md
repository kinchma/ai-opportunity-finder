# Order to delivery

**Laurel Run Trailer Works, Inc. — fictional case, as of 2026-10-31**

We sell to independent dealers, not retail customers. Dealers set retail prices and arrange or accept delivery by appointment.

## 1. Request and quote

Priya Ellwood, Dealer Account Coordinator, receives the request and checks the dealer ID and quote against the working Excel dealer list and quote records. For a new quote, she puts the dealer ID, model, dimensions and options in the Excel template. Holly Darnell, Reception and Admin Coordinator, routes calls and takes handwritten messages for Priya.

Tessa Bellamy, Dealer Sales Manager, approves the quote. Priya completes routine templates; Tessa maintains the formula/rate workbook and handles exceptional pricing or formula questions, about 100 a year. Keep the dealer's written acceptance with the email in Outlook. A quote is an offer until accepted. Check the recorded credit status before applying terms: net 30 from shipment invoice is standard for dealers approved for credit; an unspecified status does not mean approval or denial.

## 2. Make the order and schedule it

Once the dealer accepts in writing, Priya makes the dealer order in the Excel order register. She re-keys the approved model and options into the register and the paper work-order build packet. Quotes, dealer orders and work orders have distinct `QTE`, `ORD` and `WO` identifiers. Jonah Sutter, Production Scheduler, assigns a unique WO to each trailer, even when one dealer order covers several trailers, and gives it a target date.

Each week Jonah checks approved orders, stock notes and supervisor input. He sequences WOs in his Excel schedule and on the board, then gives the paper packets to the floor. The case year had 1,500 trailers completed and shipped, with one WO per trailer; roughly 30 were completed per working week over 50 working weeks. A target date is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## 3. Build, changes and inspection

Theo Lowry, Fabrication Supervisor, and his crew fabricate; the packet goes through paint and on to Avery Carver, Assembly Supervisor, and the assembly crew. Theo and Avery report progress and material exceptions to Jonah, who updates the schedule. If a target moves, Jonah tells Priya so she can update the dealer.

For a specification change, Tessa gets dealer approval. Jonah issues a dated replacement packet page; Theo and Avery return the old page, and Iris Calder, Quality and Warranty Lead, checks the current revision. There are 24 examples a year where a changed page is checked against an older copy. Do not substitute material without Felix Harrow, Manufacturing Engineer, and Iris confirming fit and required specifications. Brakes, tires, couplers, lighting and substitutions need fit and applicable compliance review. The completed trailer's certification and load labels give its GVWR and payload; an axle rating alone does not establish payload.

Iris and the finish staff check and sign the final inspection. The paper inspection checklist stays with the WO packet. Flag an inspection concern to Iris before shipping.

## 4. Ship, invoice and answer status calls

Mateo Renshaw, Shipping Coordinator, schedules shipment, notes delivery and sends shipping confirmation to Elena Corwin, Accounting Clerk. Elena issues the dealer invoice in QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise at shipment. Shipment transfers control under the fictional sales terms; an accepted order is not yet revenue. There was no separately charged freight in the case year, and sales tax collected is excluded from revenue.

For a status request, Priya checks the order register and asks Jonah or Mateo for the current position before replying. About 300 dealer status follow-ups a year are handled separately from stockout inquiries and warranty cases.

## One case example

Cedar Bend Trailer Sales, `DLR-0001`, requested `QTE-2026-1180` on 2026-10-14: one 6×12 Trailfield at $4,800 plus a $120 spare-tire mount **only**, total $4,920. Priya prepared it and Tessa approved it. The dealer accepted in writing on 2026-10-15; `ORD-2026-1120` and `WO-2026-1240` were dated that day, and Jonah scheduled the WO. Finish inspection was 2026-10-26. Shipment and invoice `INV-2026-1161` followed on 2026-10-27 for $4,920, net 30; no separate freight charge, with sales tax excluded from the amount shown. This unit is included in October's 50 Trailfield shipments, alongside 45 Enclosure and 30 Workspan shipments.
