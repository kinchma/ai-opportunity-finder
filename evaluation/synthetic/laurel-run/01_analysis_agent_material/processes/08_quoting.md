# Quoting

**Laurel Run Trailer Works, Inc. — fictional case, as of 2026-10-31**

We quote independent dealers, not retail buyers. Priya Ellwood, Dealer Account Coordinator, handles the requests and routine quote template. Tessa Bellamy, Dealer Sales Manager, owns the Excel pricing/formula workbook and approves quotes. Dealers set their own retail prices.

## From request to quote

1. **Get the details.** Priya takes the dealer request through Outlook or a call, checks the dealer ID against the Excel dealer list, and captures the model, dimensions and requested options in the Excel quote template. Check the quantity too. Holly Darnell, Reception and Admin Coordinator, routes calls and takes handwritten messages when needed. The dealer list and Outlook are the working contact records. Zoho CRM has three licensed seats under a fictional $3,600/year agreement, but Priya has entered only a small pilot set of contacts there.

2. **Work up the price.** Priya can fill in the routine template. The fictional standard dealer base prices are $4,800 for a 6×12 Trailfield, $9,600 for a 7×14 Enclosure and $12,000 for an 82-inch × 18-foot Workspan, before options. Examples of separately priced options are a spare-tire mount only for $120, an upgraded jack for $250 and an interior tie-down package for $180. Use only the options requested; the mount does not include a spare tire. These examples aren't the whole option catalog.

   Tessa maintains and understands the formula/rate logic; Priya's use of the template doesn't make her the formula owner. Priya sends exceptional pricing or formula questions to Tessa. Tessa handles approximately 100 of these questions a year. If the answer or her approval isn't available yet, leave the quote pending for Tessa rather than inventing a price or treating someone else as the approver.

3. **Review and send.** Tessa approves the quote, then Priya sends the dated quote to the dealer. Give it a `QTE-YYYY-NNNN` identifier from the quote sequence. A dated quote is an offer until the dealer accepts it in writing; it isn't an order or shipment revenue. Retain the dealer's written approval with the Outlook email. For approved-credit dealers, standard terms are net 30 days from the shipment invoice. A credit status not specified in the dealer list doesn't mean the dealer has been declined or approved; don't assume either.

## After dealer acceptance

Priya checks the dealer ID and approved quote, then starts a separate `ORD-YYYY-NNNN` record in the Excel order register. She re-keys the approved model and options into the order register and the paper work-order build packet. Jonah Sutter, Production Scheduler, assigns a separate `WO-YYYY-NNNN` for each trailer and a target date, then schedules the work. The QTE, ORD and WO identifiers are different sequences. For the year 2025-11-01 through 2026-10-31, Laurel Run completed and shipped 1,500 trailers; each trailer has its own WO.

If a specification changes after approval, Tessa obtains the dealer's approval. Jonah issues a dated replacement packet page; Theo Lowry, Fabrication Supervisor, and Avery Carver, Assembly Supervisor, return the old page, and Iris Calder, Quality and Warranty Lead, checks the current revision. Don't make an unapproved substitution. Where a component substitution is proposed, Felix Harrow, Manufacturing Engineer, and Iris must confirm fit and required specifications. Brakes, tires, couplers and lighting also need fit and applicable compliance review; don't promise a legal conclusion. The completed trailer's certification/load label gives its GVWR and payload—an axle rating alone doesn't establish payload.

## One completed example

On 2026-10-14, DLR-0001 Cedar Bend Trailer Sales requested QTE-2026-1180 for one Trailfield with the spare-tire mount only. Priya prepared it and Tessa approved $4,800 + $120 = **$4,920**. The dealer accepted in writing on 2026-10-15, creating ORD-2026-1120 and WO-2026-1240; Jonah scheduled the unit. Finish inspection was 2026-10-26. It shipped and was invoiced on 2026-10-27 for $4,920, net 30 from the shipment invoice. The stated amount excludes sales tax and has no separate freight charge. All of those example events had happened by the snapshot date.
