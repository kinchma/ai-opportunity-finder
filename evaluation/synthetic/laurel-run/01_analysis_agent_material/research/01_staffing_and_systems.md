# Staffing and systems for a 50-person Pennsylvania trailer manufacturer

A plausible dealer-sold trailer manufacturer is production-heavy, with a small office combining dealer sales, purchasing, accounting and people administration. It can operate with accounting software, spreadsheets, email and paper even without an integrated manufacturing system. That is an **illustrative operating model**, not a description or measured average of a particular company. Product mix, degree of customization, outsourcing and management preferences can change both the staffing and systems substantially. [\[1\]](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/naics4_336200.htm) [\[2\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/careers/) [\[3\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Belmont-Outside-Sales-Representative-1.pdf) [\[4\]](https://hiringcafe.com/job/purchasing-manager-pine-hill-trailers-gap-pennsylvania-cdl4m53iy5tmlq5f)

## Organization and staffing

The closest broad benchmark is national, not Pennsylvania- or trailer-only: in May 2023, production occupations accounted for 65.21% of employment in motor-vehicle body and trailer manufacturing across employers of all sizes. Assemblers and fabricators and welders were prominent occupational groups. This does not establish an exact mix for a 50-person business. [\[1\]](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/naics4_336200.htm)

For planning purposes only, one internally consistent **50-employee illustration** is:

- **35 shop personnel**, including two working team leads and fabricators/welders, cutters, painters and assemblers. Belmont Trailers in Leola advertises welder, assembler and painter roles; its assembler job covers wiring and lighting as well as floors, walls and exterior panels. [\[2\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/careers/) [\[5\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Production-Assembler.pdf)
- **4 materials and logistics personnel**, covering purchasing, receiving, stock and shipping. A purchasing-manager posting for Pine Hill Manufacturing in Gap combines supplier management, purchase orders, receiving, inventory and shipment coordination with sales and production. It mentions an ERP but does not establish the size or systems of a generic 50-person manufacturer. [\[4\]](https://hiringcafe.com/job/purchasing-manager-pine-hill-trailers-gap-pennsylvania-cdl4m53iy5tmlq5f)
- **2 engineering and quality personnel**, such as a production engineer and quality lead, with design or safety help shared or contracted where needed. Belmont advertises both production engineer and quality-control manager positions. [\[2\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/careers/)
- **3 dealer-facing personnel**, handling dealer inquiries, quotes, order entry and warranty follow-up. In Belmont’s outside-sales job description, a representative visits dealers, produces custom estimates, provides warranty and technical support, and documents activity in a customer relationship management system (CRM). [\[3\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Belmont-Outside-Sales-Representative-1.pdf)
- **3 finance and administration personnel**, combining bookkeeping, invoicing, payables, HR coordination and payroll preparation; and **3 leaders**, such as an owner/general manager, plant manager and dealer-sales manager. The counts are an inference, not a sourced roster. An outside accountant, payroll provider or IT contractor could provide support without counting as an employee. Pennsylvania explicitly describes use of third-party administrators for unemployment-tax work. [\[6\]](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dli/resources/for-employers-and-educators/how-to-file/using-a-tax-or-payroll-service)

Roles can overlap, especially quality with shop leadership and HR with finance. The owner may perform sales or finance duties directly; some shops may devote more staff to custom engineering, field sales, delivery or parts service. There is no evidence here for a universal headcount or a separate department for every function.

## Practical mix by function

**Accounting and purchasing.** A plausible baseline is desktop or cloud accounting software for the general ledger, dealer invoices, receivables, supplier bills and payments; an office employee or bookkeeper reconciles these, potentially with outside accounting support. A separate spreadsheet may hold costing, budgets or vendor comparisons. QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise is one documented product option with manufacturing features such as bills of materials, purchase orders and inventory; capabilities vary by product and edition, so its existence does not mean all QuickBooks installations provide them. [\[7\]](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/desktop/enterprise/industry-solutions/manufacturing/) A 20-person UK sauna manufacturer reported using Sage 50 and spreadsheets before adopting manufacturing software. This is an example from a different industry and country, not a Pennsylvania trailer-industry rate. [\[8\]](https://www.mrpeasy.com/blog/success-story-oceanic-saunas/)

**Dealer quoting and order entry.** Retail and wholesale responsibilities should be kept distinct. Load Trail says it routes retail quote requests to independent dealers and does not sell retail-direct; its quote form captures product line, size, axle configuration, color and options. Other manufacturers can have different channels: PROLine’s published factory-direct process states that its quotes specify model, color and options, while dealer terms may differ. [\[9\]](https://loadtrail.com/request-a-quote/) [\[10\]](https://nhtrailers.com/how-to-order/) A plausible small-shop workflow is dealer email/phone request → sales or estimator prepares a price-list/spreadsheet or software estimate → dealer-approved specification becomes an order for production. Approval, option revisions and promised date must follow the order; the precise pricing method and approval rules are assumptions, not established industry practice.

**Inventory, materials and shipping.** A buyer or materials lead could keep steel, axles, tires, lighting and other component quantities in the accounting system, a spreadsheet or a dedicated inventory tool, supplemented by physical counts and paper packing slips. Receiving staff match goods to purchase orders; shortages or revisions are communicated to the scheduler and sales. The Pine Hill role explicitly brings these functions together and calls for ERP/inventory proficiency. [\[4\]](https://hiringcafe.com/job/purchasing-manager-pine-hill-trailers-gap-pennsylvania-cdl4m53iy5tmlq5f) A trailer-specific counterexample to a manual-only assumption is Load Trail’s vendor case: its replacement ERP supported configurable product data, material planning, work orders, shipping and scanned inventory. Load Trail had an earlier ERP; the case is not evidence that a 50-person shop has or needs that product. [\[11\]](https://www.solidworks.com/story/delmiaworks/erp-success/load-trail/)

**Production and quality.** One plausible arrangement is a plant manager or working lead sequencing confirmed orders on a spreadsheet or whiteboard, with printed build sheets, drawings or inspection checklists traveling to welding, paint and assembly. Completion or shortages must then be entered or relayed back to the office. A 22-person UK precision-engineering case described an older Microsoft Access data system and paper trail before an ERP change; it supports the possibility of hybrid records, not prevalence in trailers. [\[12\]](https://www.mrpeasy.com/blog/case-study-mrpeasy-a-wrate-engineering/) More integrated shops may use a manufacturing resource planning or ERP system for bills of materials, work orders, stock and schedule. The important uncertainty is which copy of a revised specification is authoritative and whether real-time shop-floor reporting actually exists. [\[11\]](https://www.solidworks.com/story/delmiaworks/erp-success/load-trail/) [\[12\]](https://www.mrpeasy.com/blog/case-study-mrpeasy-a-wrate-engineering/)

**HR and payroll.** A small office can collect paper timecards or electronic clock records, have supervisors approve hours and send them to an outside payroll service or payroll software. In an older vendor case involving an unnamed cabinet shop—not a trailer manufacturer—employees filled in paper timecards, supervisors approved them, a payroll manager re-entered the data, and managers scheduled employees in Excel. [\[13\]](https://www3.swipeclock.com/partner-connection-blog/case-study-cabinet-shop/) In Pennsylvania, covered employers file quarterly unemployment wage and tax reports electronically; employers with 100 or fewer employees may manually enter wage detail in the state portal. Local earned-income and local-services tax withholding also requires attention to applicable employee and worksite information. The IRS says employment-tax records generally must be kept for at least four years after fourth-quarter filing; other record requirements may be longer. [\[14\]](https://www.pa.gov/services/dli/file-unemployment-compensation-quarterly-wage-tax-reports) [\[15\]](https://dced.pa.gov/local-government/local-income-tax-information/instructions-employers/) [\[16\]](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employment-tax-recordkeeping)

**Dealer CRM and service.** A simple dealer-contact spreadsheet plus email and phone is a plausible alternative to a dedicated CRM; it might track dealer contacts, territories, quote follow-ups, orders, delivery issues and warranty status. This is an inference, not a documented installation. At the more structured end, Belmont’s sales job explicitly calls for CRM visit and sales records, while Rally Trailer’s portal says it manages leads, lot inventory and orders. Belmont’s public warranty form requests dealer details, trailer model and VIN, failure information and optional attachments. None of these examples establishes that all dealer manufacturers have a portal or a CRM. [\[3\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Belmont-Outside-Sales-Representative-1.pdf) [\[17\]](https://rallytrailermfg.com/dealer-portal/) [\[18\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/warranty/)

## What the evidence can and cannot establish

In a 2026 review of **70 consenting MRPeasy customer case studies**, the vendor reported that before adopting its ERP, 64% used spreadsheets, 50% accounting software and 18% paper-based systems; companies could use more than one. The sample covered several industries and countries, with a median of 15 employees among the 55 with known employee counts. These are selected vendor customers, potentially weighted toward successful implementations—not a representative estimate for Pennsylvania, trailer makers or 50-person firms. [\[19\]](https://www.mrpeasy.com/blog/erp-statistics-manufacturing/)

Other vendor cases and product pages likewise select customers and market a solution, so they are best used to illustrate possible workflows and capabilities, not software popularity or independently verified performance gains. Primary manufacturer job postings and forms show real roles and information requests but do not establish actual staffing ratios or the firm's complete internal tech stack. [\[2\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/careers/) [\[3\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Belmont-Outside-Sales-Representative-1.pdf) [\[11\]](https://www.solidworks.com/story/delmiaworks/erp-success/load-trail/) [\[18\]](https://belmonttrailers.com/warranty/)

A useful operational inference is that handoffs—not just the brand of software—matter: dealer specification and revisions flow to the shop; material availability flows to scheduling; completion and shipment flow to invoicing; and approved time flows to payroll. Where those records are split between systems, paper and spreadsheets, stale copies or manual re-entry are possible; the exact failure frequency cannot be inferred from the sources.
