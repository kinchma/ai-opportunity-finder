# Marketing and social media

**Fictional case; snapshot 2026-10-31.** Every Laurel Run activity, count, audience figure and interaction below is invented. Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are real platform names, but this document describes no actual company account or real platform result. No account handles or addresses are supplied. The reporting year is 2025-11-01 through 2026-10-31, inclusive.

## People and routine

Keira Morrow (E010), Marketing and Dealer Support Coordinator, reports to Tessa Bellamy (E003), Dealer Sales Manager. Keira's roster allocation is approximately 70% marketing, 15% customer service, 10% sales and 5% facilities/admin. Alongside social activity, she prepares product sheets and dealer handouts in Word, maintains an Excel campaign calendar and distribution/reply notes, and communicates with dealers in Outlook. She uses approved product information and photos for social posts. Tessa approves marketing material, including posts, before publication. Keira publishes approved material and retains the approval with her working files.

Laurel Run's website has company and product information; dealer communications and printed handouts are also part of the routine. Social posts are generally product photos, short product descriptions or dealer announcements. The company channel inventory does not list a company LinkedIn or YouTube account. This says nothing about independent dealers' own channels.

## Inquiries and records

Keira checks the two company social accounts and answers product or availability questions there. The counts above refer to separate inbound product/availability message threads, not every comment, spam message or dealer operational email; later messages in an existing thread are not new inquiry threads. Business days are Monday through Friday excluding company holidays. All 17 counted threads received an initial reply within two business days and by the snapshot. The two threads for which no dealer contact was given asked only for product information. A person asking where to buy receives current dealer contact information from the company dealer list; Keira does not quote a retail price. With the person's permission, she also forwards their contact and inquiry to a dealer via Outlook. The six forwarded threads are a subset of the 15 threads given dealer contact information, not additional inquiries. Referrals and the counted replies and forwards occurred in the same month as their initial inquiry.

Dealers are independent: they set retail prices and handle retail purchases. A referral or forwarded message is not a confirmed dealer visit or purchase, and a dealer's retail sale is not Laurel Run's sale to the dealer.

Keira's monthly activity sheet records posts, month-end follower snapshots, qualifying incoming threads, timely initial replies, dealer contact information given, and consented forwards. She reviews the two platforms and the related saved correspondence, then copies and reconciles these counts in Excel and files a monthly recap for Tessa. Her recap time is recorded separately from creating posts, replying to inquiries, forwarding inquiries and preparing dealer handouts. The 12 recorded recap entries total 540 minutes; each month, including months without posts, has a recap. The October sheet is current through the snapshot.

The company sheet does not retain systematic organic reach, impressions, engagement, video views, website-traffic source or link-click measures, and it does not record later dealer follow-through, retail conversions or social-attributed manufacturer orders or revenue. The absence of a recorded outcome is not proof that no outcome occurred. Organic reach and lead conversion are separate matters and need not move together.
