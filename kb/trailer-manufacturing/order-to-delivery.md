Quote and order intake is a common bottleneck for custom trailer builders. Customers send specifications by email, phone or dealer portal, and staff re-type them into a quoting sheet and then into the production system. Automating the extraction of options, dimensions and quantities from incoming requests, with a person confirming the result, shortens quote turnaround and removes transcription errors.

Order status inquiries consume sales and customer-service time. Dealers and customers repeatedly ask where an order is in the build. An assistant that answers from the production schedule and shipping records removes most of these calls while leaving exceptions for staff.

Dealer support is another repeatable workload: spec questions, parts lookups, warranty rules and paperwork requirements. A question-answering assistant grounded in the company's own manuals, price lists and warranty policy can handle the routine questions and hand off the rest.

Invoice and purchase-order handling involves matching supplier invoices to purchase orders and receiving records for steel, aluminum, axles, lighting and components. Classifying invoices and flagging mismatches for review reduces manual matching work in accounts payable.

Warranty and quality claims arrive with photos and free-text descriptions. Triage that classifies the claim type, checks coverage, and routes it to the right person speeds responses and builds a record of recurring defects for engineering.

Production scheduling and material planning depend on accurate demand and lead-time information. Improving the data feeding the schedule is usually a precondition for any optimization, so start with visibility into orders, inventory and supplier lead times before attempting automated scheduling.
