Keep agents away from safety-critical decisions. Weight ratings, structural specifications and regulatory compliance need engineering sign-off, so use AI to prepare and check paperwork, not to decide specifications.

Integrations with the ERP or accounting system are the main effort and risk in most manufacturing automations. Prefer starting with read-only access, such as order status lookups, before allowing an agent to write records.

Plan for seasonality. Demand swings in many trailer markets, so a pilot should run long enough to cover both busy and slow periods before drawing conclusions about savings.
